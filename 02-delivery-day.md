# Delivery Day

## How to Approach It

- [ ] Budget real time — a thorough pass through this list easily takes
      90+ minutes. Don't rush because staff seem eager to move you along;
      you don't have to leave the lot until you're satisfied.
- [ ] If possible, pick up at an express checkout site — Tesla parks the
      car and leaves paperwork on the dash, giving you unhurried time to
      inspect before signing. Note: express sites are often staffed by
      temporary/low-level staff who can't resolve problems on the spot —
      issues found there usually mean scheduling a follow-up appointment.
- [ ] Don't schedule pickup at dusk or later — you need daylight (or a
      flashlight, see pre-delivery) to actually see defects.
- [ ] **Avoid end-of-quarter delivery if you can** — Tesla pushes hard to
      hit quarterly delivery numbers in the last 2–3 weeks of March, June,
      September, and December. The result: delivery centers get overwhelmed,
      demo inventory disappears, paperwork isn't ready, cars arrive
      uncharged, and staff are stretched thin. The quality of the
      experience (and sometimes the car) drops noticeably. If your window
      is flexible, aim for early-to-mid quarter.
- [ ] Don't sign final documents or wire remaining funds until you've
      completed a full inspection — once signed, you've accepted the car
      as-is for anything outside warranty coverage.
- [ ] **You can refuse delivery if quality is genuinely inadequate** — you'll
      be assigned a new VIN. This is a practical right at pickup, not a
      timed return window: Tesla's 7-day return policy was discontinued in
      2020 and no equivalent exists today. Once you sign and drive off, your
      recourse is warranty claims and service appointments, not returns.
- [ ] **Report cosmetic damage before you leave** — scratches, paint defects,
      and panel issues are far easier to get addressed as pre-delivery
      findings than as warranty claims filed later. Document everything with
      photos on-site. Items covered under the Basic Vehicle warranty remain
      claimable regardless of when you notice them.
- [ ] **If you notice cosmetic damage after leaving, report it promptly via
      the Tesla app** — Tesla has a post-delivery window for cosmetic issues;
      the sooner you contact them the better. Don't assume it's too late just
      because you've driven off the lot.

## Verify Records Match Your Order

- [ ] Vehicle VIN matches your paperwork and the vehicle's own VIN.
      The VIN's physical location varies by factory: windshield (driver
      side bottom) and door jamb label on all cars; Fremont builds also
      on the RH upper B-pillar; Shanghai builds under the RH front seat;
      Berlin builds on the RH rear door sill. Decode key digits:
      - **Digit 7** — battery chemistry: **E** = Li-ion (NMC/ternary),
        **F** = LFP
      - **Digit 10** — model year (US/Fremont/Austin) or calendar year
        (Shanghai/Berlin): **S** = 2025, **T** = 2026, **V** = 2027
      - **Digit 11** — factory: **A** = Austin TX, **B** = Berlin,
        **C** = Shanghai, **F** = Fremont CA
- [ ] Full name and address on paperwork are correct.
- [ ] Vehicle's "About" screen (car icon, bottom-left → Software) shows
      nothing that doesn't match the vehicle. If ordering AWD, confirm
      "All-Wheel Drive" appears here — verifies both motors are in the
      build, not just the paperwork.
- [ ] Monroney record matches your configuration.
- [ ] Color, interior, and wheel style match what you ordered. Current
      Juniper-refresh (2025+) wheel options are 19" Crossflow or 20"
      Helix 2.0; pre-refresh cars used 19" Gemini or 20" Induction — check
      against your actual order confirmation, not just this list, since
      names change across model years.
- [ ] A sleeve with two keys is provided.
- [ ] Odometer reading is low and consistent with a factory-fresh car
      (a few miles for transport is normal; a notably high reading can
      indicate a demo, loaner, or service-loaner unit — ask if unsure).
- [ ] Check whether the battery pack is 2170 or 4680 cells: car icon →
      Software → tap "Additional Vehicle Information" — don't just ask
      the delivery advisor or assume by trim, confirm on-screen. 2170
      (Panasonic) has been the standard for US-delivered Model Y and has
      a faster Supercharger curve. 4680 has a notably slower one (10-80%
      in 40+ min) and was reintroduced into some Model Y production in
      early 2026 as a supply-chain/tariff hedge — unconfirmed how widely
      this actually reaches US retail deliveries, so verify on-screen
      rather than assume either way. Quick VIN shortcut: digit 7 also
      tells you battery chemistry (**E** = Li-ion/NMC, **F** = LFP) —
      readable before you even get in the car.

## Exterior — Before You're Given Access (locked)

- [ ] Headlight fit.
- [ ] Paint around the front bumper's intake vents.
- [ ] Frunk: paint defects, dents, or signs of improper closing near the T
      logo.
- [ ] Hood sits flush with quarter panels and frunk.
- [ ] Windshield installed properly — gaps between glass and A-pillar
      should be roughly equal on both sides. An uneven install causes
      noticeable wind noise at highway speed.
- [ ] Panel gaps are even and panels sit flush overall.
- [ ] Bottom side trim is flush with the bottom of the doors; check for
      trim stains.
- [ ] All 4 door levers function.
- [ ] Wheels aren't scratched; nothing is lodged in the tires (screws,
      nails, debris).
- [ ] Tire pressures match the door-jamb placard (factory/transport
      overinflation is common — check with your own gauge if unsure of
      the delivery center's).
- [ ] All 4 hubcaps present and properly attached.
- [ ] No dings on pillars.
- [ ] Paint and door jambs — no chipped panel corners.
- [ ] Black rocker panels aren't damaged.
- [ ] Tail light fit — full-width light-bar design on current Juniper-era
      (2025+) cars, discrete lamp on pre-refresh cars.
      > Known issue (pre-refresh cars only): tail light (passenger side)
      > not flush with panel — no standard fix as of the 2024 source. The
      > redesigned Juniper-era full-width taillight doesn't have this
      > specific defect reported; if buying pre-refresh inventory, still
      > worth checking.
- [ ] Charging port fit (driver's side).
- [ ] Tail lights for condensation.
- [ ] Undercarriage — look for scratches, dents, damage, missing
      components, missing screws, or screws sticking out.
      > Fix: needs a 10mm wrench — ask the service center to inspect all
      > undercarriage screws before you leave.
- [ ] Wheel wells — each has noise-dampener covers held by plastic pins
      (pushed fully in) and metal threaded pins (visibly sticking out)
      with plastic nuts. Confirm no empty pin holes, and all metal pins
      present (2 per front wheel well, 4 per rear) with nuts attached.
      > Fix: mobile service can correct this in minutes if pins/nuts are
      > missing.
- [ ] Alignment/gaps around headlights, trunk arms, roof, frunk.

## Exterior — Panel Gaps & Paint Walkaround (doors closed)

Do a full lap, checking for misalignment, gaps, scrapes, dents, overspray,
or underspray — trim and door handles included:

- [ ] Front of frunk aligns with the bumper.
- [ ] Trunk arms align with left/right quarter panels.
- [ ] Headlights and rear lights are flush with the body.
- [ ] Charging port flush with the body.
- [ ] Front bumper, right front quarter panel, right front wheel, right
      passenger door, right rear door, right rear wheel, right rear
      quarter panel, trunk, rear bumper, left rear quarter panel, left
      rear wheel, left rear door, driver's door, left front wheel, left
      front quarter panel, frunk — each individually, for scrapes/damage.

## Exterior — With All Doors, Frunk, and Trunk Open

- [ ] Power liftgate operates and height-adjusts correctly (sometimes the
      weather seal catches in the molding, causing a click).
- [ ] All doors (including frunk/trunk) open and close without unusual
      effort or sound.
- [ ] Door windows auto-close completely when doors close.
- [ ] Frunk/trunk lighting works (2 trunk lights).
- [ ] Check paint in normally-hidden areas: door hinges, bottom of
      frunk/trunk, etc.
- [ ] Turn on all exterior lights: fog, headlights (low/high beam,
      checking for condensation inside the housing), tail lights, reverse,
      brake (checking for condensation), turn signals (front/left/right/
      rear), running lights.
- [ ] Wipers function correctly; check hood for wiper dings.
- [ ] Charge port door opens/closes correctly via manual tap, the screen/
      app, and the charger button; charge port indicator light works.

## Glass & Seals

- [ ] All glass, including side mirrors: no distortion, ripples,
      scratches, or cracks.
- [ ] No trim scratches on windows — a software bug reported in the 2024
      source can skip auto-lowering the window when opening the door,
      which causes this. *(Verify this bug is still current — likely
      software-patchable.)*
- [ ] Weather seals around door gaps and windows are intact.
- [ ] Water guards below the rear windshield are clear.
- [ ] Windshield and windshield trim properly installed.
- [ ] Roof glass panel and rear window edges are clean.
- [ ] Rubber seal is properly attached at the bottom of all 4 doors
      (Tesla calls it a "secondary seal").
      > Known issue: loose seal on driver door bottom — fix is a service
      > center replacement.

## Interior

**Sit in the driver's seat:**

- [ ] No error messages on the display.
- [ ] All 4 windows roll fully up and auto-up/down correctly; front two
      roll fully down, rear two go down most but not all the way — that's
      normal by design (they'd otherwise have to travel into the wheel
      well), not a defect.
- [ ] Rearview camera works.
- [ ] Side mirrors fold and adjust position correctly; no warping or
      distortion in the rearview mirror.
- [ ] Steering wheel position controls work; horn works (press center).
- [ ] Sun visors, visor mirror, and visor lighting work.
- [ ] Front overhead driver/passenger lights, center console slide +
      compartment lights, glovebox open/close + lighting all work.
      > Confirm the USB drive for Sentry Mode / Dashcam is present in the
      > glovebox. If it hasn't been pre-formatted by Tesla, format it
      > before leaving: Controls > Safety > Format USB Drive. Formatting
      > erases any existing content — do it before you put anything on
      > the drive.
- [ ] All seat position controls work; front seats don't squeak in any
      direction of motion. Check driver-seat lumbar support adjustment.
      Passenger-side lumbar adjustment may not exist on Juniper-era
      (2025+) cars — don't assume it's there; verify what your specific
      trim actually includes.
- [ ] Heated seats and seat warmers work; footwell lighting works.
- [ ] Front seat ventilation works, if equipped (Juniper-era front seats
      add ventilation in addition to heating).
- [ ] Ambient lighting strip cycles through colors/settings correctly, if
      equipped (Juniper-era cabin).
- [ ] Steering wheel control lights work (may need enabling in Driver
      settings).
- [ ] Wireless phone charging works on both charging pad positions.
- [ ] Wired charging: 2x USB-C front, 2x USB-C rear, and the 12V DC
      receptacle all have power.
- [ ] HVAC — run hot and cold at full power and listen for strange
      noises; forcing the heat pump to kick in (temperature to either
      extreme) is a good stress test.
- [ ] Park Assist — press brake, shift to Drive/Reverse, and slowly approach
      a fixed obstacle (cone, bollard, wall) to confirm proximity warnings
      appear on screen.
- [ ] Blind Spot camera — enable Automatic Blind Spot Camera, signal a
      lane change, confirm the camera image appears (as of 2026 firmware
      this shows on the instrument cluster next to the speedometer, not
      just the center screen). On Juniper-era cars with ambient lighting,
      the entire cabin light strip on the relevant side pulses red when
      something is detected in the blind spot (spring 2026 update). The
      door-open version of this also works while parked: if a cyclist or
      car is approaching, the lights flash red and the door won't unlatch
      on the first press — verify this works at delivery.
- [ ] Turn signal stalk actuates smoothly.
- [ ] Gear selection works correctly (reverse, neutral, park). Pre-refresh
      cars: steering-column shift stalk. Juniper-era (2025+) cars: swipe
      up/down on the touchscreen (chime confirms each shift); also confirm
      the backup "P" button on the overhead console engages Park if the
      touchscreen is unavailable.
- [ ] Metal accelerator and brake pedal arms — some units have mismatched
      finish (matte accelerator, glossy brake) and a visible weld on the
      brake pedal arm; inspect if this concerns you.
- [ ] Sound system — play a familiar track, check all speakers via
      balance/fader, listen for unexpected clicks.
- [ ] Toybox → Sketchpad app: leave it open for a minute and watch for
      phantom touches or dead pixels.

**Elsewhere in the cabin:**

- [ ] Screen, center console, and dash — no scratches or dings.
- [ ] Headliner — no grease, damage, or scuffs anywhere around it.
- [ ] Both coat hooks extend, retract, and latch.
- [ ] Steering wheel — no scratches or tears, front and back.
- [ ] Door sills — no scratches.
- [ ] Seats — no scuffs, damage, or bad seams; verify third-row seats
      work correctly if ordered; confirm the middle rear seat sits flush
      at both ends; back seats recline correctly.
- [ ] Floor mats — no rips or stains.

**Get out of the driver's seat:**

- [ ] Driver and passenger door emergency releases work gently.
- [ ] Puddle lights (driver/passenger), rear door pocket lighting, door
      latch button lights, door window button lights all work.
- [ ] Front, rear, and middle seatbelts operate properly.
- [ ] Rear left/right clothing hook operation in the back seat area.
- [ ] Rear seats fold down correctly via seat/trunk buttons (power-folding
      on Juniper-era cars — listen for correct motor operation), and don't
      catch on the rear trunk floor carpet when folding.
- [ ] Rear passenger touchscreen (8", Juniper-era) powers on and responds
      to touch, if equipped.
- [ ] Lift the bottom of the back seats — confirm it's securely attached
      to its base.
- [ ] Rear left/right overhead lights work.

## Frunk

- [ ] Opens and closes properly.
- [ ] Trim clips around the frunk are all attached.
- [ ] Tow hook is included (stowed under the frunk mat).
- [ ] Cargo covers (if any) are properly attached.
- [ ] Rear seat covers are properly attached.

## Charging

- [ ] Vehicle accepts a charge at the delivery-center Supercharger, if
      available.
- [ ] Mobile Connector (with NEMA 5-15/14-50 adapters) and J1772 adapter
      are included — check under the frunk mat or in the trunk. Standard
      on North American deliveries as of the Juniper refresh regardless
      of trim, but confirm rather than assume — international deliveries
      and the refresh transition period have shown some variation.
- [ ] Express Pickup note: cars leave the factory around ~50% charge; the
      local delivery location should charge to 80–90% before handoff —
      confirm this if picking up same-day.

## Lighting Summary

(Cross-check against the exterior sections above — this is the full list
in one place if doing a dedicated lighting pass.)

- [ ] Front headlights (low/high beam) — alignment, no condensation.
- [ ] Front park lights, front fog lights.
- [ ] Turn on hazards: front, left/right, and rear turn signals; rear
      running lights; rear brake lights — no condensation in housings.

## Connectivity / Pairing

- [ ] Both key cards work with the vehicle — keep one on your person in
      case the phone app misbehaves.
- [ ] Smartphone pairs over Bluetooth; music streaming, unlock, etc. work.

## Upcoming Features

Tesla regularly announces and rolls out new features to Model Y. This section
is for features announced or recently in rollout that may not yet be standard
across all configurations and production timing. As features become universally
available and documented, they migrate into the sections above.

**At delivery, check software version and capabilities**: car icon > Software >
About (note the version); Controls > Service. Availability varies by trim,
configuration, and production timing — a missing feature isn't a defect if
it hasn't reached your build yet.

**Full sourcing and status history:** `upcoming-features.md`

### Verify at delivery

- [ ] **16" center display** — standard on all US Model Y as of Oct 1, 2026
      (pre-Oct builds shipped with 15.4"). The upgrade also bumped resolution
      from 1080p to QHD (1440p). Confirm the main screen is the larger 16"
      panel; check Controls > About if unsure.
- [ ] **V2L (vehicle-to-load) hardware** — standard on US Model Y Premium
      since Aug 2026. Allows the car to power external devices (tools,
      camping, power outage backup) via an adapter. Hardware must be present
      in the build — not retrofittable — so confirm before accepting delivery.
- [ ] **Front camera sealed housing + auto-cleaning** — in Juniper Model Y
      production since ~Jan 2026. Sealed anti-fog housing with washer-fluid
      pulse + air-burst cleaning when the lens is occluded. Physical
      hardware tied to build date, not a software update — verify your VIN
      includes it (ask the delivery rep or check build paperwork).

### Watch tier (not yet on US consumer Model Y)

- [ ] **Side-repeater camera airflow pod** — passive sculpted housing that
      sheds water/grime from side camera lenses. Cybertruck only as of
      Sep 2026; no announcement for consumer Model Y yet.
- [ ] **Zen Grey interior** — replaces white on Premium trims. Live in China
      and Europe (Aug 2026); not yet on US Model Y Premium. The US
      configurator still showing white interior = changeover hasn't hit yet.
- [ ] **Longer wheelbase on standard 5-seat Model Y** — unconfirmed rumor
      that Tesla will migrate the standard Model Y (not just the L 6-seater)
      to the longer wheelbase platform. No official announcement; low
      confidence. Monitor for any Tesla production or configurator changes.
