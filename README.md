## Scope

- **Model**: 2025+ Tesla Model Y Juniper refresh (US delivery)
- **Hardware**: HW4/AI4
- **Trims covered**: RWD and AWD notes where they differ

Items are Juniper-era specific where it matters — pre-refresh gotchas are flagged as such.

The **core checklist** (root files) focuses on things that matter before, during, and immediately
after taking delivery — missed defects, critical security and charging setup, and ongoing
maintenance habits. The **`tips/` folder** covers operational knowledge that makes day-to-day
ownership better: recommended settings, feature guides, and voice command reference.

**Out of scope:** general EV buying advice, model comparisons, deep financing/leasing guidance,
non-Tesla vehicles, and maintenance/repair procedures beyond "here's what to watch for and when
to get service involved." Basic financing mechanics (pre-approval, state availability, trade-in
math) are covered at a checklist level — not as financial advice.
