# Pre-Delivery

## Before You Buy

- [ ] **Get pre-approved for financing before you place your order** —
      Tesla's checkout will prompt you for financing details, and having
      a pre-approval in hand lets you compare Tesla's rate against your
      own bank or credit union before committing.
- [ ] **Verify Tesla financing is available in your state before ordering**
      — Tesla's lending partner doesn't operate in every state. Check
      Tesla's financing availability page before you place your order. If
      your state isn't supported, call Tesla support after ordering to
      arrange delivery in a supported state. Example: South Carolina
      residents may need to take delivery in Georgia or North Carolina.
- [ ] **If your credit is frozen, unfreeze it before applying** — the
      pre-approval process will flag a frozen file and give you a direct
      link to the relevant credit bureau to lift it. You're typically
      given the option to unfreeze temporarily for several days rather
      than permanently.
- [ ] **Save a screenshot of your pre-approval** — keep it with your
      purchase records.
- [ ] **Use a referral link when placing your order** — you'll receive
      free months of FSD. Referral benefits change over time; verify
      what's current at order time. If you don't have one, use:
      https://ts.la/ron824452 *(the author of this checklist also
      receives a referral benefit when this link is used)*
- [ ] **Set the correct zip code on the order screen** — Tesla uses your
      zip code to assign a delivery location. Buy at the location you
      intend to pick up from; a showroom sales rep isn't on the ground
      at the pickup location and can't inspect the vehicle before selling
      it to you — requests just get forwarded and details get lost in
      the handoff.

## Home Charging Prep

- [ ] **Order your Level 2 charger before you have a Tesla in your account**
      — Tesla's app store requires an existing vehicle in "My Products"
      to purchase a Tesla Wall Connector. If you try to order through the
      app before delivery, you'll be blocked. Workarounds:
      - **Best Buy** carries the Tesla Wall Connector at price-matched
        Tesla.com pricing — the easiest option before delivery.
      - **Amazon** also carries it but typically at a higher price.
      - Tesla support confirmed this restriction; it is not a bug.
- [ ] **Schedule your electrician early** — Tesla maintains a network of
      recommended electricians accessible through the Tesla app or website.
      Installation slots fill up; booking before your delivery date means
      you're charging at full speed from day one rather than waiting weeks
      on a Level 1 trickle charge. For reference: the Tesla Universal Wall
      Connector ran ~$600 and installation (50A breaker + wall mount) ran
      ~$600 as of late 2026 — verify current pricing, as the charger alone
      saw a ~10% price increase within weeks of that purchase.

## Tracking Your Order

- [ ] **Watch the "Edit Design" link in Vehicle Details** — on your Tesla
      order page, the Vehicle Details section shows an "Edit Design" link
      (back to the configurator) while your order is queued. When that
      changes to "You cannot edit design at this time," your vehicle has
      entered production. Your VIN should appear in that same section
      shortly after.
- [ ] **Don't read too much into the EDD until the car is in transit** —
      the Estimated Delivery Date typically won't update to a tight window
      until the vehicle is actually moving toward its final destination.
      Wide or shifting EDDs before that point are normal, not a signal
      something is wrong.

## Once Your VIN Is Assigned

- [ ] **Look up your exact build in Tesla's parts catalog** — go to
      service.tesla.com, open the Parts Catalog, enter your VIN, and
      select "Original Fitment." It shows every part Tesla built into
      your specific car. Useful for verifying hardware that's not
      retrofittable: confirm the **SuperManifold V2** is listed if you
      ordered V2L/power-share capability, or check the front drive unit
      designation on AWD models. The catalog distinguishes builds — if a
      part shows as "Original VIN Part," that hardware is in your car.
      For AWD front motor specifically: look for **TL1** (current US
      spec, 142 kW) vs **TL2** (new spec, 176 kW — confirmed in China
      and Australia as of Oct 2026, not yet in US production).
      Low-confidence tip: a small number of owners report that VIN digit
      8 = **U** correlates with a TL2 Parts Catalog entry, while **K**
      correlates with TL1. Not officially documented — use the Parts
      Catalog to confirm, not the VIN alone.

## Things to Bring to Delivery

- [ ] If the vehicle will be registered to more than one person, everyone
      on the registration needs to attend to sign final documents.
- [ ] Driver's license.
- [ ] Smartphone with the Tesla app installed and signed in.
- [ ] Written or digital proof of down payment and final payment details.
      If financing, confirm the loan is funded before arriving and bring
      proof you can show if asked.
- [ ] Proof of insurance — and confirm your policy has been updated to
      this vehicle's VIN before you leave for pickup. Easy to add "a new
      Tesla" to a policy without the correct VIN, which can leave you
      uninsured on the drive home.
- [ ] **Shop around for insurance before your VIN is assigned** — rates
      for a Model Y vary significantly between carriers, and switching
      can save meaningful money. Two things to watch for: some insurers
      offer an attractive introductory rate and quietly raise it at the
      6-month renewal, counting on inertia to keep you. And the cheapest
      quote isn't always the goal — the cheapest quote from a reputable
      carrier is. Those are not always the same company. Check ratings
      (J.D. Power, AM Best) alongside the premium.
- [ ] Trade-in car, if applicable.
- [ ] USB-A, USB-C, and 12V DC cables for testing the car's ports.
- [ ] Flashlight — delivery/inspection areas can be dark.
- [ ] Any accessories you want to install immediately.
- [ ] **Buy track tape (3M or similar) before pickup** — if you don't have
      PPF scheduled yet, apply a strip over the lower front bumper and
      leading edge of the hood before leaving the lot. Protects against
      road chips on the highway drive home. Peel off when you arrive.
- [ ] Texas deliveries: bring the original Texas Vehicle Inspection Report.
