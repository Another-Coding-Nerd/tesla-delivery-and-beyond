# Feature Guide

Features worth knowing about as you settle into ownership. Not setup
checklists — these are here when you need them.

## Juniper-Specific (2025+)

- [ ] **The ambient light strip signals blind spots** — when your turn
      signal is on and something is detected in that blind spot, the entire
      interior accent strip on that side pulses bright red. Also works when
      parked: if a cyclist is approaching and you reach for the door handle,
      the lights flash red, the car chimes, and the door won't open on the
      first press. Spring 2026 feature — nothing to configure, just know
      it's there.
- [ ] **Rear passengers have an interactive map on the back screen** — the
      rear touchscreen shows the active navigation route in real time;
      passengers can pinch to zoom, pan, and check the ETA without asking
      the driver. Worth pointing out on road trips.

## Climate & Comfort

- [ ] **Use Pet Mode when leaving animals in the car** (tap the fan icon >
      Pet Mode) — keeps the cabin at a safe temperature and displays a
      message on the touchscreen: "My owner will be back soon. Don't worry,
      the AC is on and I'm comfortable." That message is intentional — it
      stops concerned passersby from breaking a window to rescue an animal
      that's actually fine. Can also be activated remotely via the app
      while you're away after leaving the animal inside.
- [ ] **Use Camp Mode for overnight stays in the car** (same fan icon menu)
      — keeps climate, lights, USB power, and air circulation running
      without triggering the alarm or the car going to sleep. Screen dims
      to a low-light mode. The Model Y cargo area with seats folded flat
      is long enough to sleep in comfortably.

## Navigation

- [ ] **Share an address to Tesla nav from any app** — find a destination
      in Google Maps, Apple Maps, a text message, or any website, tap
      Share, and select the Tesla app. The address loads in the car's
      navigation instantly with no touchscreen typing.
- [ ] **Swipe right on the nav bar to instantly route home or work** —
      from the map screen, a single right swipe on the navigation bar
      starts routing to your saved home address (or work, if you're at
      home). Requires home and work addresses saved in navigation settings.
      Fastest way to start a commute.

## Full Self-Driving & Navigation Tips

- [ ] **Pick your route before tapping Go** — after entering a destination,
      Tesla shows multiple route options on screen. Most people tap Go on
      the first suggestion. Tap the routes list first and pick the one
      you'd actually drive — it takes 5 seconds and saves frustration later.
- [ ] **Drop a pin when parking location matters** — searching for a
      destination lets Tesla choose where in the lot to park. To control
      the specific spot: zoom in on the map, long-press to drop a pin where
      you actually want to stop, and navigate to the pin instead. Can be
      saved to favorites for places you park regularly. Known quirk: pins
      occasionally drift on their own and need to be reset.
- [ ] **Cancel and re-enter the destination mid-trip if you don't like the
      route** — if FSD picks a route you'd never choose, cancel navigation
      and re-enter the same destination; the car recalculates and often
      presents different options. Works especially well when you're closer
      to the problematic section of the route. The car typically keeps
      driving during the recalculation, but be ready to take over if FSD
      disengages at the moment you cancel nav.
- [ ] **Use your turn signal to nudge FSD's direction** — at slow speeds or
      stopped at an intersection, engaging the turn signal sometimes
      convinces FSD to go that way rather than its planned route. Not
      reliable at highway speeds and won't override a strong nav intent, but
      worth trying at a turn where it's being stubborn.
- [ ] **Start FSD without a destination to influence parking lot exits** —
      if FSD consistently exits a lot the wrong way regardless of your
      destination, enable FSD before entering a destination. The car picks
      an exit direction based on immediate context; once it commits to the
      right way, then enter the destination. Works most of the time.
