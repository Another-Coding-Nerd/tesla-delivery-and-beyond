# App Checklist — Verbatim Transcription

Transcribed from screenshots in this directory (section01-1.png through section07-2-section08.png).
Source: Tesla delivery checklist app.

---

## Before you sign (6 of 6)

- [ ] **VIN lines up**
      Read the VIN on the glass, in the app, and on the purchase papers.
      All three should match before you sign.

- [ ] **Insurance is active**
      Confirm the policy is in force today and lists this VIN. Bring proof
      you can show at handover.

- [ ] **Payment is settled**
      Down payment, trade-in, and loan or lease papers should be signed,
      with nothing still marked due.

- [ ] **Full Self-Driving transfer**
      If you are moving Full Self-Driving from another Tesla, start that
      transfer before the final signature.

- [ ] **Write down what you want fixed**
      Note paint, fit, or missing parts you want addressed, and agree them
      before you sign the delivery papers.

- [ ] **Note the mileage**
      Record the odometer at handover so later questions about transport or
      cosmetics have a starting point.

---

## Surface and fit (6 of 6)

- [ ] **Paint in open light**
      Walk the car in daylight. Look for chips, scratches, and rough spots
      in the clear coat.

- [ ] **Even panel gaps**
      Compare the gaps around the hood, doors, and rear opening. They
      should look even from side to side.

- [ ] **Handles and charge door sit flush**
      Run a hand over the door handles and the charge-port door. Nothing
      should stick out or sit in a pocket.

- [ ] **Glass and rubber seals**
      Check the windshield and roof glass for cracks at the edges, and
      make sure the seals are not folded over.

- [ ] **Lamps and moisture**
      Look into the front and rear lamps. A light haze can clear. Standing
      water or a crack should be noted.

- [ ] **Light-bar seams**
      The front and rear light bars should sit even in their housings, with
      no popped clips or dark segments.

---

## Clockwise body walk (8 of 8)

- [ ] **Nose and front trunk**
      Front bumper, hood paint, and the nose camera. Stay on the outside
      for this pass.

- [ ] **Passenger side**
      Same pass on the passenger side: doors, mirror, sill, and the area
      around the charge port.

- [ ] **Tail and cargo opening**
      Rear bumper, lamps, and the trunk or tailgate. Open it and check that
      the latch and weather seal sit properly.

- [ ] **Driver side**
      Driver doors, mirror, and the sill. Look along the body in raking
      light for dents.

- [ ] **Roof and glass**
      Roof panel or glass, the seals around it, and the antenna base. Look
      for chips from transport.

- [ ] **Quick look underneath**
      From the side, glance at the aero panel and rocker for scrapes. Do
      not crawl under the car at handover.

- [ ] **Front trunk tub**
      Open the front trunk. The liner should sit flat, the latch should
      catch, and the emergency release should be visible.

- [ ] **Cargo floor**
      Lift the cargo floor. The panel underneath should be seated, and any
      included tray should be in the car.

---

## Cabin (7 of 7)

- [ ] **Seats and headliner**
      Check the seat surfaces and the headliner for scuffs, loose
      stitching, or stains.

- [ ] **Rear cushion locks**
      Lift each rear cushion and let it drop. It should latch and not rock.

- [ ] **Center screen**
      Look for stuck pixels. Drag a finger across the screen and confirm
      the touch follows you.

- [ ] **Cabin light strips**
      Turn the cabin light on. The strips should meet cleanly across the
      dash and doors, including the rear of the cabin.

- [ ] **Rear display**
      Wake the rear screen. It should respond without a frozen image or a
      dark corner.

- [ ] **Seat, wheel, and mirrors**
      Move the driver seat, steering wheel, and mirrors through their range
      and back.

- [ ] **Child-seat anchors**
      Find the lower anchors and the top tether. They should be reachable
      and easy to spot.

---

## Keys, sound, and ports (6 of 6)

- [ ] **Phone as key**
      Lock by walking away and unlock by walking up. Both should happen
      without opening the app.

- [ ] **Key cards**
      Tap each card on the door pillar and on the console reader. Both
      cards should wake the car.

- [ ] **Speakers**
      Play music and move the balance left, right, front, and rear. Each
      side should sound, with no buzz.

- [ ] **USB ports and charge pads**
      Plug into each USB-C port you can reach and set a phone on each
      wireless pad.

- [ ] **Rear USB layout**
      On Model Y, confirm the rear-seat USB-C ports are where you expect
      them and that each one charges.

- [ ] **Car in the app**
      The car should appear in the Tesla app. Note the software version
      shown on the screen.

---

## Cabin systems (6 of 6)

- [ ] **Heat and cold**
      Run the climate to full heat, then full cold. Listen for rattles, and
      confirm air reaches the vents.

- [ ] **Manual door releases**
      Find the mechanical release in each door and confirm you can reach it.

- [ ] **Door swing**
      Open and close every door twice. They should latch fully and not rub
      the seal.

- [ ] **Wipers and washers**
      Spray the windshield and run the wipers. Fluid should reach the
      glass, and the blades should clear it.

- [ ] **Horn, indicators, hazards**
      Tap the horn, flash the indicators both ways, and switch the hazards
      on and off.

- [ ] **Camera glass**
      The windshield area in front of the forward cameras should be clean,
      with no film or haze left from transport.

---

## Charge and kit (5 of 5)

- [ ] **Charge-port door**
      Open it from the app, the screen, and the port button. It should
      open, close, and sit flush.

- [ ] **Tow eye**
      Find the tow eye in the front or rear storage well. You want it in
      the car before you leave.

- [ ] **Dashcam drive**
      If a USB drive was included for Sentry Mode, it should be seated in
      the glovebox port.

- [ ] **J1772 adapter**
      The adapter for J1772 public stations should be in the mobile
      charging bag.

- [ ] **NACS inlet**
      Confirm the charge inlet is the NACS shape you expect, and that any
      included adapter matches it.

---

## Wheels (2 of 2)

- [ ] **Tire pressures**
      Compare each tire with the pressures on the door placard. They should
      be close, not flat from storage.

- [ ] **Tire repair kit**
      Find the inflator or sealant kit in the cargo well. There is no spare
      wheel.
